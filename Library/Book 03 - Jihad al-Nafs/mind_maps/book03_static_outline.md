# Book 03 — Conceptual Map Outline

## The book's five movements

1. **Formation (Units 01–05):** education, natural drives, the human spiritual dimension, jihad al-nafs, and the commanding self.
2. **Discipline (Units 06–08):** patience as an action, patience as a character trait, and the consequences of patience.
3. **Repair and social self-control (Units 09–14):** repentance, delayed repentance, repair, inner breadth, and disciplined speech.
4. **Devotion and means (Units 15–22):** servitude, activity and perseverance, sincerity, night prayer, Qur'an, supplication, and recourse to Ahl al-Bayt.
5. **Orientation (Units 23–25):** remembrance, reliance, and the stages of tawhid and tawakkul.

## Major conceptual hubs

The canonical graph identifies **u17 (ikhlas)** as the strongest hub, followed by **u15 (servitude to God)**, **u06/u07 (patience)**, **u03 (jihad al-nafs)**, and **u16 (activity, perseverance, and correction)**. This means the book should not be read as a simple linear list of virtues: sincerity, servitude, discipline, and practical perseverance repeatedly connect otherwise separate chapters.

## Reading question

A useful synthesis question is: **Does the book's final teaching on tawhid and tawakkul merely add a theological conclusion, or does it reinterpret the disciplines introduced earlier?** Trace at least three bridges from the final units back into the earlier formation and discipline units.
