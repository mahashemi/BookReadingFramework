Study Guide — Jihad bil-Nafs

A serious question bank for two kinds of readers: students who want source-grounded retrieval, and ordinary readers who naturally ask, “What does this actually mean in my life?”

Recommended review cycle

Answer from memory first; reveal the guide only afterward. Revisit on Day 1, 3, 7, 16, and 35. On later passes, explain each answer in your own words and give a concrete example.
