### High-value cross-book arrows

- **Introduction → al-Hassan:** circumstance-sensitive interpretation.
- **'Ali → al-Hassan:** restraint can be principled, not cowardice.
- **al-Hassan → al-Sadiq:** political opening must be tested against real conditions.
- **Zayn al-'Abidin → Musa al-Kazim:** spiritual influence can be a form of resistance.
- **al-Rida → al-'Askari:** political pressure can require conditional participation or concealment.
- **al-'Askari → al-Mahdi:** surveillance surrounds the expected continuation of the Imamate.
- **Universal Justice → Mahdism:** future justice is linked to a broader account of human perfection.
- **'Ali's Kharijite lesson → Mahdism:** historical claims require investigation; belief should not erase critical judgment.
