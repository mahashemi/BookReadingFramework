\begin{frame}{One Test, Four Very Different Answers}
\centering\scriptsize
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabular}{|p{3.4cm}|p{3.1cm}|p{3.6cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Choice} & \textbf{Degree of cooperation} & \textbf{Why it still passes the test} \\\hline
al-Rida's crown-princeship & Highest (formal title, stripped of function) & Non-interference condition; refused every substantive function \\\hline
al-Hassan's peace & High (negotiated settlement) & Genuine circumstantial conditions for a truce were met \\\hline
al-Sadiq's refusal & Low (declines offered power) & Offer failed the sincerity/feasibility test before cooperation began \\\hline
Musa al-Kazim's refusal & Lowest (rejects all accommodation) & No confession, even costless ones, that would legitimize the ruler \\\hline
\end{tabular}
\vspace{0.3cm}
\begin{center}
\Large\boxed{\text{Test} = \text{Does this serve genuine religious purpose, or self-interest?}}
\end{center}
\end{frame}

  \item Why is the Kharijite episode a warning about superficial religiosity rather than simple villainy?
  \item Why can al-Hassan's peace and al-Husayn's revolt both be principled, given the same underlying law?
  \item Give one example of spiritual resistance from Chapter 3 or Chapter 5, and say what it actually resisted.
  \item Why did al-Sadiq reject the Abbasid political opening without rejecting the underlying cause?
  \item What single negotiated condition does the whole defense of al-Rida rest on?
  \item What does al-'Askari's chapter demonstrate about the limits of state surveillance?
  \item How does universal justice connect to Mahdism as one continuous argument?
  \item Why does ``awaiting'' not cancel any ordinary religious duty?
  \item Reconstruct the whole-book chain, in one paragraph, in your own words.
\end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
