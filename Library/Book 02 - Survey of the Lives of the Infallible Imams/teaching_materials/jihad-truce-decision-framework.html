Imam al-Hasan (peace be upon him) · truce

  1. He was the sitting caliph, so his death would carry a different political consequence.
  2. Kufah’s force was unreliable and fragmented.
  3. A conditional treaty was available, including protective terms and no pledge of allegiance.
  4. The truce could expose Mu‘awiyah’s conduct while limiting immediate Muslim bloodshed.

Imam al-Husayn (peace be upon him) · uprising

  1. He was not a sitting caliph; the chapter contrasts his position with al-Hasan’s.
  2. His small group of companions remained loyal.
  3. No conditional truce was available on the same terms; allegiance was demanded.
  4. The chapter presents manifest political and religious wrongdoing as part of the context for his stand.
