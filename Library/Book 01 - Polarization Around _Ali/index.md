# Book 01 — Polarization Around the Character of 'Ali ibn Abi Talib
### Ayatullah Murtadha Mutahhari

## Summary
Mutahhari argues that attraction and repulsion is a universal law running through creation, and that 'Ali ibn Abi Talib embodies both powers more completely than any figure in history — drawing love that survived his death by centuries, while also confronting three distinct kinds of deviation (greed, political hypocrisy, and fanatical false piety) during his caliphate. The book's two parts examine, in turn, whom 'Ali attracted and why, then whom he repelled (chiefly the Khawarij) and why.

## Author & Context
Murtadha Mutahhari (1920–1979) was a leading modern Iranian Islamic scholar and student of 'Allamah Tabataba'i. This book began as four lectures delivered at the Husayniyah al-Irshad in Tehran in 1969.
