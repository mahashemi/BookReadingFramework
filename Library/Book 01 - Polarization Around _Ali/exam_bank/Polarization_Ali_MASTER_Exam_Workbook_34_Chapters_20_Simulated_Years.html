CHAPTER 01 / 34

The Law of Attraction and Repulsion

Mutahhari begins with a general law: attraction and repulsion operate throughout creation. In human social life, the subject is not sexual attraction or merely material cooperation, but friendship, affection, enmity and hatred. He links these reactions to resemblance and contrariety, while also discussing human need and benefit. Attraction and repulsion are presented as fundamental to an ordered human life.
