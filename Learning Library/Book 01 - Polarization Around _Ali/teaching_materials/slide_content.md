# Slide Content — Claim, Punchy Bullets & Examples Merged
### Mutahhari's method: state a general claim, then prove it with a story or image. Each slide below has: the **claim** (verbatim quote — your main slide text), **punchy bullets** (short, memorable phrasing for visual impact), and the **example** Mutahhari uses to prove the claim (what you narrate live).

---

## Slide 0 — Recap
**Heading: Last Time — The Law That Governs Everything**

**Claim (quote):** *"Not a single atom of the world of existence is outside the governance of general attraction."*
- Every atom obeys attraction
- No two people are close without a hidden "why"
- Four kinds of people: rock · magnet · thorn · both
- 'Ali: one man, two powers

**Example used:** Rumi's raven and stork — both secretly lame in one leg

**Say aloud:** Recap fast — these claims are the seed of everything today builds on.

---

## Slide 1 — Opening
**Heading: Love and Hate Are Not Opposites — They're One Faith**

**Claim (hadith, Al-Kāfi Ch. 60, Hadith 5):** *"Is belief anything but love and hate?"* — Imam al-Ṣādiq (a.s.)

**Claim (hadith, Al-Kāfi Ch. 60, Hadith 6):** *"The strongest ring of belief to hold onto... is love for the sake of Allah, hate for the sake of Allah."* — the Prophet (s)
- Faith made *beloved*. Disbelief made *hateful*. (Qur'an 49:7)
- Not prayer alone. Not fasting alone. Not hajj alone.

**Example used:** companions guess prayer, zakat, fasting, hajj, jihad — all wrong; the Prophet corrects them

**Say aloud:** These two hadiths ARE your opening — the exchange itself is the story, no separate example needed.

## Slide 2 — Powerful Attractions
**Heading: A Call That Never Stopped Ringing**

**Claim (quote):** *"'Ali's power of attraction was [three-dimensional]... not limited to one or two centuries."*
- One-dimensional → fades. Two-dimensional → lasts. Three-dimensional → reaches the heart.
- 'Ali's attraction: the mark of a prophet's call

**Example 1:** Maytham at-Tammar — praised 'Ali from his own cross, 20 years later
**Example 2:** Ibn as-Sikkit — lost his tongue rather than flatter a caliph, 200 years later

**Claim (quote):** *"If 'Ali had had no divine colouring... he would have been forgotten."*
- "He became more alive."

**Claim (quote):** *"'Ali is like the laws of nature which remain unchanged by time. He is a well-spring of munificence which is never dry."*

**Say aloud:** Tell both stories in full — claim, proof, claim, closing image. Follow that order live.

## Slide 3 — Shi'ism, the Religion of Love
**Heading: Love That Survives the Sword**

**Claim (quote):** *"One of the greatest marks of distinction of Shi'ism... is that its foundation and its bedrock is love."*
- "'Ali and his party will be the triumphant ones." — the Prophet (s)
- "The believer will never be your enemy, the hypocrite will never love you."

**Claim (quote):** *"'Ali is the standard and criterion for assaying human nature and temperaments."*
- His hand was cut off. He praised 'Ali anyway.

**Example used:** the man whose hand was cut off — *"Why should I not extol him, now that his friendship is mixed with flesh and blood?"*

**Say aloud:** Build to that last line as the punchline — it's the emotional peak of this slide's story.

---

## Slide 4 — The Elixir of Love
**Heading: Love Is the Real Philosopher's Stone**

**Claim (quote):** *"Love, absolutely, is the 'elexir'... which changes one nature into another."*
- Copper into gold. Bitterness into sweetness.

**Claim (quote):** *"Love is the power of glory, it makes the coward courageous."*
- **Example:** the hen — timid alone, fierce with chicks to defend

**Claim (quote):** *"Love makes the heavy and lazy nimble and cunning, and even makes the slow-witted astute."*
- **Example:** the once-lazy adolescent, the once-sleepy new mother
- "The nightingale learnt its song by the favour of the rose."

**Claim (quote):** *"In the spirit, the effect of love is... development and thriving; in the body... melting and decomposition."*
- Destroys the body. Perfects the spirit.

**Say aloud:** Pick 2 claim+example pairs to narrate in full (hen + one more) — the rest can stay as slide text only.

---

## Slide 5 — Breaking Down the Barriers
**Heading: Love Is What Breaks You Out of Yourself**

**Claim (quote):** *"Self-love is a limitation and a defensive barrier; love completely breaks down this defensive barrier."*
- Timid. Cut off. Alone. — until love breaks the wall.

**Claim (quote):** *"The struggle with self-love is the struggle with the limitations of the self."*
- Not less self — a *bigger* self
- "The personality must expand to take in every other human being."

**Claim (quote):** *"The reform of man does not lie in reducing him, it lies in perfecting and adding to him."*
- "Whoever's garment is torn by love is cleansed of every blemish."

**Say aloud:** Clarify live that this isn't about hating yourself — it's expansion, not erasure. Walk the three claims in order: barrier → struggle → what reform actually means.

---

## Slide 6 — Closing
**Heading: The Well That Never Runs Dry**

**Claim (quote):** *"He is like the laws of nature which remain unchanged by time — a well-spring which is never dry, but which rather increases day by day."*
- Love and hate: not a contradiction — a completed faith
- 'Ali: love strong enough to outlive centuries

**Say aloud:** End on that final quote alone — let it be the last thing said, no summary line after it.
