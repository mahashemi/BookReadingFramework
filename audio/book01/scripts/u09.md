# Listen & Learn — Breaking Down the Barriers

## Narration

This section considers the barriers that separate people and the ways love can overcome them. The key is not to ignore moral differences, but to understand how a sincere bond can loosen pride, inherited hostility, and narrow self-interest.

### Self-love is a limitation and a defensive barrier

Self-love is a limitation and a defensive barrier. Until a man goes outside himself he is timid, avaricious, covetous, misanthropic, quick-tempered, selfish, arrogant; his spirit gives out no spark, has no vivacity, is 'always cold and cut off'. As soon as he steps outside, these qualities are destroyed.

Crucial clarification: self-love in the sense of something to be eliminated does not really exist. It makes no sense for a human being to try not to like himself. Amour-propre — esteem for oneself — has not been mistakenly overlooked such that we must throw it out.

### The reform of man does not lie in reducing him; it lies in perfecting and adding to him

The reform of man does not lie in reducing him; it lies in perfecting and adding to him. Creation's assignment to man is in the direction of the course of creation — perfection and growth, not decrease and reduction.

Therefore the struggle with self-love is the struggle with the limitations of the self. The defensive structure that treats everything not connected to the individual as foreign, 'not me', alien, must be broken down. ## Deep Reading Insight

Removing barriers does not mean removing discernment. A generous heart can welcome people while still refusing to normalize injustice or excuse harmful conduct.
