THEIR VIEW OF THE CALIPHATE — 'the only idea of the Khawarij's that could be interpreted favourably by the modern thinkers of today'. ### Development: apparently this was not their original view

Development: apparently this was not their original view. Their slogan la hukma illa li'llah, and Nahj al-Balaghah (Sermon 40; Ibn Abi'l-Hadid vol.2 p.308), indicate they first believed society needed no leader or government at all — people should put the Book of God into practice on their own. ### Their historical verdicts: the caliphates of Abu Bakr and 'Umar were rightful — rightfully elected, never deviating from the best interest

Their historical verdicts: the caliphates of Abu Bakr and 'Umar were rightful — rightfully elected, never deviating from the best interest. 'Uthman's election was rightful, but toward the end of his sixth year he changed direction and ignored the Muslims' interest; he should have been deposed, and since he continued in office he was killed as an unbeliever and his killing was a religious duty. 'Ali's election was rightful, but since he accepted arbitration and did not repent, he too was killed as an unbeliever and his killing was a religious duty. So: they denounced 'Uthman's caliphate after its seventh year and 'Ali's after the arbitration. They abhorred all subsequent caliphs and were always at war with them. (Source: al-Shahrastani, al-Milal wa'l-Nihal.)

### THE DECLINE

THE DECLINE. ## Deep Reading Insight

A political theory must be assessed not only by its stated ideals but by the judgments and actions it permits. Watch how a claim about faith or leadership affects the treatment of those who disagree.
