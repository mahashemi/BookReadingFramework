# Listen & Learn — 'Ali — One Man with Two Powers

## Narration

This section brings the two forces together in the character of Ali. The point is not that a person must be gentle in every situation or severe in every situation. A principled character can attract through compassion and repel through resistance to injustice, without contradicting itself.

We will move through the concepts in sequence. Listen for the way each point contributes to the larger argument, and for the difference between what the source reports and what it asks the reader to infer.

## Deep Reading Insight

Consistency does not mean using the same outward behavior in every circumstance. It means applying the same underlying principles—mercy, justice, honesty, and responsibility—even when those principles require different responses.
