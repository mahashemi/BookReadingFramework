# Listen & Learn — Just a Motto? — and 'Ali's Sense of Democracy

## Narration

This section distinguishes a motto from the spirit that should animate it. A person may repeat the right formula while failing to live by its moral substance; conversely, the deeper test of a tradition is the way it teaches people to recognize and follow truth.

Applied to Sunni/Shi'ah. Shi'ah motto: 'Ali is caliph immediately after the Prophet, designated by divine decree. ### The original schism, re-described

The original schism, re-described. The majority 'only looked at the superficial aspect', were 'not sufficiently sharp-sighted or penetrating', and 'found the most convenient solution': great men and elders took a certain way, and it cannot be said they erred. The minority said they would respect anyone who respected the truth, but where they saw the fundamentals of Islam violated at the hands of the very people who had long served Islam, they would respect them no longer. 'They said they were partisans of the principles of Islam, not partisans of the personalities of Islam.' Shi'ism was born in this spirit.

THE DOUBTING COMPANION AT JAMAL. ### IBN AL-KAWWA' IN THE PRAYER

IBN AL-KAWWA' IN THE PRAYER. They would not pray behind 'Ali, considering him a disbeliever, but came to the mosque to molest him. As 'Ali stood in prayer with the people behind him, Ibn al-Kawwa' shouted Qur'an 39:65 — 'if thou associatest, thy work shall surely fail and thou wilt be among the losers' — insinuating that 'Ali's past services (the brotherhood, laylat al-mabit when he slept in the Prophet's bed as a lure for swords) had been cancelled by his unbelief. WHAT CHANGED. 'In the beginning the Khawarij were peaceable, and contented themselves with merely criticizing and speaking openly.' As they despaired of 'Ali repenting, they resolved on revolution. NAHRAWAN. ## Deep Reading Insight

A tradition is measured by its spirit in action. The author asks readers to judge people by truth rather than judge truth by the status or reputation of the people who claim it.
