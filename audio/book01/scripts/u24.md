### The criterion restated (Ch

The criterion restated (Ch. 4): having both powers is not itself a virtue — Genghis Khan, Hajjaj and Mu'awiyah had both. What certifies leadership is who is attracted and who is repelled. 'Ali attracted Maytham, Ibn as-Sikkit, Sa'sa'ah, the man whose hand he amputated; he repelled the money-worshippers of Jamal, the schemers of Siffin, and the fanatics of Nahrawan.
