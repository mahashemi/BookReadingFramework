### Scope: the discussion is restricted to his somewhat more than four-year caliphate — though 'Ali was always two-powered

Scope: the discussion is restricted to his somewhat more than four-year caliphate — though 'Ali was always two-powered. From the beginning of the Islamic age one group gravitated to him and another 'were occasionally pained by his existence'. The caliphate simply gave both powers greater manifestation, since before it his links with society — and so his attraction and repulsion — were fewer.

### THE YEMEN GARMENTS INCIDENT

THE YEMEN GARMENTS INCIDENT. 'Ali commanded an army to Yemen; returning toward Mecca he left a deputy and hurried ahead to report. The deputy distributed the garments among the soldiers so they could enter Mecca in new clothes. 'Ali objected and reproved him: no decision should have been taken before the Prophet's orders — in 'Ali's eyes this was expropriation from the bayt al-mal without the leader's permission. He ordered the garments removed and stored. The soldiers complained to the Prophet of 'Ali's harshness. The Prophet: 'O men, do not grumble about 'Ali. I swear by Allah that he is more intensely in the way of God than that anyone should complain about him.'

## Deep Reading Insight

Opposition is not automatically proof of virtue, just as popularity is not proof of truth. The task is to understand the reasons for conflict and judge them by evidence and principle.
