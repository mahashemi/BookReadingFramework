# Listen & Learn — The Law of Attraction and Repulsion

## Narration

This section introduces attraction and repulsion as two forces that shape relationships and moral life. The question is not merely why people are drawn to one another or pushed apart, but how these forces can help or harm a person depending on what they are attached to and what they reject.

We will move through the concepts in sequence. Listen for the way each point contributes to the larger argument, and for the difference between what the source reports and what it asks the reader to infer.

## Deep Reading Insight

Attraction and repulsion are powers, not moral verdicts. Before praising either one, ask what is attracting us, what is being rejected, and what kind of person these responses are making us become.
